## 0.2.0

- **Where from? lists the places you've used**, the busiest first, so the same shop doesn't end up on the list
  under two names. Typing narrows the list to the closest places (the typed part in bold); a tap, or the arrow keys
  and Enter, picks one. It replaces the browser's own suggestions, which some browsers and the companion app
  didn't show.
- **A place is matched however it's typed**: case, spaces and punctuation don't count, so "woolies" is Woolies and
  "on-line" is Online. Text that's nearly a place you have (a typo like "Wooolies", or "Woolworths" with Woolies on
  the list) or part of one isn't made a new place by Add: the closest place goes in
  the box with "Did you mean Woolies?", and Add again uses it. **New place** in the list, or **Use "…" as a new
  place** under the box, keeps what was typed. The From box when changing a thing works the same way.
- **Places side by side line up**: on a wide card, the first place in the second column sat a little higher than
  the first.

## 0.1.1

- **The count strip fits a narrow space**, such as an overlay on a picture: under 240 px wide it drops the arrow
  (it's still a link) and gives the count the room, and a count too long for the space ends in an ellipsis instead
  of running into the edge.
